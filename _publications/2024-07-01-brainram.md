We reconstruct visual stimuli observed by human subjects from fMRI signals using text-to-image diffusion models. Inspired by human memory, we retrieve semantically related textual and visual representations with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), improving reconstruction performance by 9%. The paper was accepted to ACM Multimedia 2024 (CCF-A) and selected for an Oral presentation (top 3.97%).
