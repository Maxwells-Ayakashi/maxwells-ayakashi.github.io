We reveal and quantify a major evaluation pitfall in text-to-image diffusion models: merely increasing the guidance scale can substantially improve mainstream human-preference metrics without improving actual image quality. We develop a geometric evaluation framework that applies equivalent guidance scales to classifier-free guidance (CFG) and compares it against other guidance methods, showing that most existing methods overstate their performance (their winning rate drops by 16% against equivalent CFG).
